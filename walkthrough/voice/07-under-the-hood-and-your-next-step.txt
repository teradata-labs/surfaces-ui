Under all of this is one small interface, DataSource. Use the DuckDB, DataFusion or Teradata engines, or write your own. Queries are read-only, and sorting, filtering and paging happen in sequel. Themes are tokens, so contrast is tested in every preset. The quickest way in is the playground. Clone the repo, run p n p m install and p n p m dev, pick a dataset, and start poking at it. Thanks for watching.
