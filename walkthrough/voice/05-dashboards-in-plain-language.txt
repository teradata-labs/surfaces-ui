Pages are documents too. Ask for a change in plain words and the agent edits the page. Each instruction is one undo step, and the log says who made it. This one is built by hand in React. Click a bar and every other view filters. The filter lives in the address bar, so you can share the link. And the filtering happens in the database, never in the browser. The charts follow Tufte and Few. Bars start at zero, there's only ever one scale, and categories are sorted largest first. If a chart's shape would mislead, it says so right underneath. Headline numbers come with a trend beside them, so a total never stands alone, and every chart has a table and a text reading too.
