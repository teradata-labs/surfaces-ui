Gummy motion. New tokens `--sf-ease-gummy` (a damped spring) and `--sf-duration-springy`, zero under reduced motion. A press springs back when let go, the segmented thumb slides with the spring and squishes where it lands, and a control that switches on or off squishes once after a pointer or a finger changed it. The NavBar's highlight slides from page to page; tree carets turn and the rows beneath glide as a branch opens; sections open to their height; a folded workspace panel drops in when unfolded; and the chat's setup menu slides between its sections.
