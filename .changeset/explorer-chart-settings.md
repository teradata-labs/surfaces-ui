`Explorer` and `ReportBuilder`: each shelf says which axis it is; the chart is drawn as bars, a line or an area; bars can be swapped onto their side; each measure can take its own kind and a right-hand scale, marked "left" or "right" on its pill (`ExploreState.chart`, also the `chart` prop in the catalog and kept on report tiles); pills move along a shelf by drag, touch included, or Alt+arrows; a field in use can be taken off from the field list, and a person's own field removed with Shift+Delete. `Chart` takes `horizontal`.
