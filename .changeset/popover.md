`Popover`: a button that opens a small panel of controls beneath it, such as a view's width, zoom and data state behind one View button. A non-modal dialog opened by a disclosure button (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`): focus moves into the panel, Escape closes it and gives focus back to the button, a press or focus outside closes it. Placed below the button at its start or end edge and kept inside the window; controlled (`open`, `onOpenChange`) or not.
