Arranging a surface by hand is part of the library, not only the playground. `useArrange` (with `ArrangeToggle`, `ArrangeHelp` and `ArrangeGhost`) in `@surfaces-ui/spec/react` lets a person move components by dragging or by keyboard, put two side by side, and set heights and column widths, on any surface; the pure helpers behind it (`moveComponentMessages`, `stepComponentMessages`, `besideMessages`, `heightMessages`, `gridWidthsMessages` and others) are in `@surfaces-ui/spec`. What it sends is the same JSON an agent sends to arrange a page. `PageStudio` takes `arrange` (off by default): each arrangement is one undo step, logged by the person who made it, and told to the agent with the next instruction as a `layout.changed` note. The styles are in `@surfaces-ui/components/styles.css` as `sf-arrange-*`.

`editsFromMessages` no longer removes a component that a message moves from one container to another.
