A High contrast preset, and `Theme.contrast: "more"` for any theme. With it, edges reach 3:1, tertiary labels 7:1 and quaternary 4.5:1, dividers are stronger, glass is solid, and every pane and control draws its edge through the new `--sf-color-edge` token (transparent otherwise, and turned on by `prefers-contrast: more` too). Sand's accent is now a burnt orange with some brown in it, and its charts follow a new orange-led order.
